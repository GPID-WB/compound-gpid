# Quality Review Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing output.

## Source Integrity

- Every numeric claim is sourced or marked [SOURCE NEEDED: ...].
- Every uncertain claim uses [VERIFY: ...] or equivalent marker.
- No fabricated citations or dates appear in final text.

## Institutional and Sensitivity Guardrails

- Institutional-position statements are either sourced or marked for clearance.
- Unpublished or preliminary material remains explicitly labeled.
- Country-sensitive language follows approved terminology.

## Structure and Readability

- Document structure matches the requested type and audience.
- Section transitions are clear and evidence-preserving.
- Caveats and limitations remain visible.

## Format Scope

- Output is English.
- Output is Markdown or basic .qmd prose and YAML only.
- Unsupported full Quarto execution or data binding is deferred explicitly.
