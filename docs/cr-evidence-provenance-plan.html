The shared architecture

One lifecycle, many methods.

Phase 4 gives the program a spine without flattening the methods. Structural econometrics, ML, and measurement keep their existing specialist surfaces while sharing the responsible stages around them.

The decision behind the plan

Research is not just more code.

Compound GPID already provides a disciplined loop for brainstorming, planning, building, reviewing, and compounding lessons. The CR expansion adds research-native safeguards for the places where a polished answer can still be wrong: source provenance, methodological justification, normative choices, and comparability.

responsible research partner

The value-first sequence

The approved direction is deliberately phased. Evidence and provenance come first because they support every later research archetype; measurement and normative gates then address the decisions that shape policy-facing results.

The order is the safeguard.Each phase adds capability only after the previous phase gives it a place to be checked, challenged, and maintained.

What “good” feels like

  • A reader can follow a claim back to a source.
  • A researcher can see which method choice the evidence supports.
  • A ranking carries sensitivity and comparability context.
  • A value judgment is visible and human-approved.
01 / corpusLocal first

Documents in the working repository are the default research corpus.

02 / truthOriginal first

Converted Markdown helps indexing; the original document remains the authority.

03 / integrityAbstain early

Never invent a source, DOI, quote, page, or locator to make prose sound complete.

04 / methodEvidence explains

Evidence must justify methodological choices, not only statements in the final prose.

The central design

One claim. One trail.

The spine separates analysis from composition. Analysis builds verified evidence records; composition can use those records but cannot quietly invent its own support.

The hard stop

When evidence is missing, the answer pauses.

This is the operational heart of the plan. The system does not turn uncertainty into confident prose. It marks the gap, records it, and routes it for resolution.

Scope discipline

What this phase does not try to solve.

Keeping the boundaries visible is part of responsible planning. The four phases cover the responsible-research backbone; they do not pretend to be the research itself.

in / out

In this program

  • Evidence and provenance contracts.
  • Measurement and classification integrity.
  • Scoping and normative-decision gates.
  • Lifecycle cross-linking and method packs.

Still out of scope

  • The actual Beyond-GDP or measurement analysis.
  • An autonomous external-literature-search backend.
  • A team evidence library and second-use-case validation.
  • New method packs beyond structural, ML, and measurement.
One deliberate constraintMarkitdown remains an optional ingestion convenience, not a required runtime dependency. Original documents remain authoritative, and Phase 4 never moves agent files.
