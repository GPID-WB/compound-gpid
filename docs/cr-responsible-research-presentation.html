Compound ResearchMeeting brief / 2026.07.31

Research needs a different contract with AI.

The CR approach treats AI-assisted research as a governed process for thinking, evidence, judgment, and communication, not as a faster way to produce plausible text.

The propositionMake it easier to explore, harder to silently overclaim, and easier to audit later.
01 / PhilosophyWhy research cannot inherit a coding workflow unchanged

AI reduces the cost of plausibility.

It does not reduce the cost of truth. In research, that gap is where the serious failures live.

Fluent output is not evidence that the underlying research is correct.
01
Evidence must be inspectable

Claims need a source, a locator, and a verification status.

02
Judgment must be visible

Normative and methodological choices cannot disappear into defaults.

03
Uncertainty is an output

Abstention, limits, and competing explanations are useful research artifacts.

02 / PhilosophyThe failure modes are different

A generic code review cannot see every research error.

The most dangerous mistakes are often silent: the code runs, the prose reads well, and the result is still wrong or overstated.

Invented authorityEvidence riskMade-up citations, page numbers, quotations, or publication details that look scholarly.
Method driftTheory riskThe code implements a subtly different model, estimand, or functional form than the derivation.
Specification searchIntegrity riskMany models are run, but only the favorable specification reaches the paper.
Default valuesNormative riskThresholds, weights, exclusions, or frames are chosen without an explicit human decision.
Confident communicationOutput riskThe table or narrative is stronger than the evidence, caveats, or uncertainty support.
Design implication: research needs its own task taxonomy, artifact contracts, gates, and reviewers.
03 / PhilosophyThe design stance

Constrain the path.
Expand the thinking.

CR is not trying to make research mechanical. It is trying to reserve human attention for the decisions that deserve it.

01
Explore broadly

Surface alternative frames, methods, assumptions, and explanations.

02
Commit explicitly

03

04
Preserve for next time

05 / PhilosophyReuse the responsible parts

One backbone, many method packs.

The method changes the theory, estimator, and diagnostics. It should not change the standard for evidence, explicit judgment, or verification.

06 / The approachStart with the decision, not the estimator

Scope before method.

Before a model is selected, CR asks what decision the evidence will inform and which choices could change who is seen, ranked, or affected.

01
Problem framingPolicy question, target population, unit, time horizon, comparability.
02
Competing framesAt least two defensible ways to see the same question.
03
Theory priorsBehavioral, data, and identification assumptions before results.
04
Success criteriaSubstantive, statistical, integrity, and reproducibility standards.
Human decision register

AI surfaces the choice.
Humans own the choice.

  1. Enumerate the value-laden decision.
  2. Show defensible options and consequences.
  3. Require an explicit decision and justification.
  4. Link it to the study, plan, and output.

No silent defaults for thresholds, weights, framing, exclusions, or burden allocation.

07 / The approachSeparate analysis from composition

Evidence comes before prose.

A fluent draft is downstream of a reviewed evidence base. The writer can express verified claims, but cannot repair missing evidence by sounding confident.

Analysis

Produce structured, evidence-linked artifacts.

  • Claims, methods, results, caveats
  • Exact passages and source locators
  • Competing findings and unresolved items
  • Claim-evidence matrix

Composition

Transform reviewed material into a research output.

  • Writing brief and approved source records
  • Only claims with status: verified
  • Citations generated from evidence IDs
  • No invented source, result, quote, or certainty

08 / The approachSource authority is not optional

A source is not a citation until it can be resolved.

The original document remains the authority. Converted text is an index that makes research material searchable; it is never permission to guess.

01
Preserve the original

Keep the authority file and its repository path.

02
Record provenance

Source ID, origin, hash, version, retrieval date, converter.

03
Index for analysis

Use converted text for headings, passages, tables, and locators.

04
Verify the claim

Resolve the source and locator before the claim becomes publishable.

09 / The approachPrioritize silent errors

The review system is built around what can go wrong quietly.

CR uses a priority system that blocks silent research errors before they become results, manuscripts, or policy claims.

P0Blocking Silent errors that can produce wrong results without warning.
P1Critical Incorrect behavior that must be fixed before results are shared.
P2Important Missing robustness, documentation, or reproducibility work.
P3Advisory Alternatives and presentation improvements worth noting.

P0 examples

The system is explicit about the errors it refuses to wave through.

  • Code and derivation do not match
  • Identification claim lacks its diagnostic
  • Randomness has no explicit seed
  • Specification search is hidden
  • Claim has no verified evidence
  • Citation or locator cannot be resolved
Rule: abstain and flag beats plausible completion.

CR is not “CG plus economics vocabulary.” It is a different research contract built on CG's engineering discipline.
14 / NextIdeas for making CR more useful to researchers

Research improvements: make the partner more present at the point of judgment.

The next gains should reduce friction around real research decisions without hiding the decisions themselves.

01

Project source inventory

Let a researcher see the local evidence corpus, missing sources, versions, and external opt-ins before analysis begins.

02

Interactive evidence checkpoint

Give the user a compact review view for approving, correcting, or abstaining on claims before composition.

03

Diagnostic templates

Offer method-specific checklists and starter artifacts for IV, DiD, RDD, ML validation, and measurement sensitivity.

04

Result-to-output lineage

Link a table or figure back to the script, specification manifest, data vintage, seed, and review findings.

05

Uncertainty in the interface

Make unresolved claims, limitations, confidence, and evidence status visible in the normal working view.

06

Researcher-friendly onboarding

Explain the workflow through one realistic study journey, with examples that feel like economics rather than software setup.

16 / NextSequence the value

A pragmatic sequence for the next phase.

The order matters: first make claims traceable, then make measurement decisions robust, then deepen method packs and the user experience around them.

Now / harden

Evidence spine

Make provenance, claim verification, anti-hallucination behavior, and P0 review reliable in daily work.

Next / expand

Measurement

Add weighting sensitivity, cluster validity, threshold checks, and vintage comparability as usable artifacts.

Then / deepen

Method packs

Retrofit structural econometrics and ML with strong theory-data dialogue and diagnostic templates.

Always / compound

Communication

Connect evidence and decisions to tables, figures, writing, replication, and institutional memory.

Success test: a researcher should be able to answer “what do we know, why do we believe it, what did we choose, and what could still be wrong?” without reconstructing the session from memory.
