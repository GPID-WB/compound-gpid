# Let the agent proceed and ask afterwards

**The idea.** From pstack: on reversible work the agent decides, proceeds and reports, and stops only for actions that cannot be undone.

**Why not.** The purpose of this plugin is that the human owns and can explain the work. Two hard stops stay: approval of the plan, and sign-off. One part of the idea is kept: the agent does not ask the human for anything it can look up or run.

**What would reopen it.** Evidence from pilots that the hard stops are answered without reading. The response would be to make them shorter, not to remove the human's decision.
