# A panel of several models for every review

**The idea.** From pstack: run each review on models from different vendors and keep what they agree on.

**Why not.** It multiplies cost and waiting on every piece of work, and many analysts have access to one model. A verifier in a fresh context, required to prove each finding, is the default. A human may still ask a second model.

**What would reopen it.** Planted-violation tests showing that single-model verifiers miss what a second model catches.
